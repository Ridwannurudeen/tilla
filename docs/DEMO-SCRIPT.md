OKX.AI Genesis Hackathon · deadline Jul 27, 2026

**Description (1 sentence):** Describe what you sell, and Tilla's AI builds, brands, and deploys a real storefront on OKX X Layer with crypto checkout — live in minutes, sellable to humans and agents alike.

## SHOT LIST (0:00–1:30)

**[0:00–0:07] — THE PAIN**
Shot: Screen recording, fast jump-cuts across a cluttered desktop — a Stripe dashboard, a Webflow builder, a DNS settings page, a wallet integration doc, all flashing by in a blur.
Caption/VO: *"Launching a paid product used to take a weekend. Domain. Payments. Design. Code."*

**[0:07–0:12] — THE CUT**
Shot: Hard cut to black, then Tilla landing page fades in: `tilla.gudman.xyz`
VO: *"Now it takes one sentence."*

**[0:12–0:14] — TITLE CARD**
On-screen text: **TILLA** — describe it. sell it. today.

**[0:14–0:32] — THE MAGIC: TYPING THE PROMPT**
Shot: Screen capture of the Tilla input box on `tilla.gudman.xyz`. Cursor clicks into the field.
On-screen action: Type, live, character by character:
> "I sell a Notion productivity template for freelancers, 9 USDT"
VO (as it types): *"Just describe what you sell..."*
Beat: hit Enter / Generate button.
VO: *"...and Tilla's AI does the rest — brand, copy, palette, product page — generated on the spot."*
Shot: brief loading animation (1–2s) showing generation in progress (brand name forming, colors filling in).

**[0:32–0:48] — THE REVEAL: A REAL LIVE STORE**
Shot: Cut to the finished store, live at `tilla.gudman.xyz/s/invoice-flow/` — full-screen scroll-through: hero with "Invoice Flow" branding, product copy, palette, the "Pay 9 USDT on X Layer" checkout section.
VO: *"This isn't a mockup. It's live — a real branded storefront, deployed to its own URL, with crypto checkout wired in, in under a minute."*
On-screen text overlay (bottom third): **tilla.gudman.xyz/s/invoice-flow**

**[0:48–1:04] — THE BUYER MOMENT**
Shot: Cursor clicks "Pay 9 USDT on X Layer." Wallet popup appears, buyer confirms the transaction.
VO: *"A buyer pays in USDT, on-chain, on X Layer..."*
Shot: Quick cut to a block-explorer style confirmation / on-chain tx moment.
On-screen text: **✅ Verified on X Layer**
Beat: page updates — download/delivery unlocked.
On-screen text: **🔓 Delivery unlocked**
VO: *"...verified on-chain, and the product unlocks automatically. No manual fulfillment. No middleman."*

**[1:04–1:14] — AGENTS TOO**
Shot: Split-screen or quick cut to the OKX AI marketplace listing: **#6961 "Tilla"** — service "Create Storefront," 1 USDT via x402.
VO: *"And it doesn't just sell to people. Tilla sells to agents too — any agent can pay 1 USDT over x402 and get Tilla to build them a store."*

**[1:14–1:24] — THE PUNCHLINE**
Shot: Return to Tilla landing page, zoom out.
VO: *"One person. One sentence. A store an agent can hire, and a store agents can buy from. This is the one-person company — the unlimited workforce, built in."*
